About Irish Van Man

One van, one name, one person who picks up the phone.

Meet Derek

Irish Van Man is Derek — a Dublin removals man who got tired of hearing the same complaints about the trade. Quotes that creep up on the day. Lads who won't take a small job. Vans that turn up two hours late with no phone call.

So the business is built around the opposite of all that. You deal with Derek from the first phone call to the last box. The price you're quoted is the price you pay. And if the job is one wardrobe rather than a whole house, that's grand — it still gets booked in.

Most of the work comes from people recommending us to a neighbour or a sister-in-law, which is exactly how a removals business should grow.

How we work

🤝

Straight talking

If a job needs two people and a bigger van, we'll say so up front. If you'd be better off hiring a trailer for the afternoon, we'll say that too.

🛡️

Properly covered

Goods-in-transit and public liability insurance on every job, with certificates available on request before you book.

⏰

On time, or you'll know

We give a tight arrival window and stick to it. If the M50 has other plans, you get a phone call — not silence.

🧰

Kitted out

Blankets, straps, trolleys, floor runners, tool kit and shrink wrap travel with the van on every single job.

♻️

Sensible about waste

Clearance items that still have life in them go to a charity shop or a reuse scheme where we can, rather than straight to landfill.

📍

Dublin first, Ireland after

The van is Dublin based and out across the city every day, but it'll happily point west or south for a long-distance move.

Common questions

How far in advance should I book?

A week or two for a full house move, especially around the end of the month. Small jobs and single items can often be done within a day or two.

Do you charge extra for stairs or long carries?

No. Tell us about the stairs when you're getting the quote and the price accounts for them. Nothing gets added on the day.

Can you move on a Saturday or in the evening?

Yes, at the same rate. Weekend mornings book up fastest, so get in early if you want one.

What can't you take?

Hazardous materials, gas bottles, paint, fuel and live plants in the back of a closed van over a long distance. Everything else, ask us.

Do you take cash or card?

Both, plus bank transfer. You get a proper receipt either way.
